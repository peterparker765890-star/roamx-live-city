10 / DATA & LIMITATIONS

How ROAMX Investigates

Environmental information

Weather and air-quality estimates are retrieved from Open-Meteo services. Model outputs are not local sensor measurements.

Location discovery

Search combines Open-Meteo / GeoNames, OpenStreetMap / Photon and Esri. Public map indexes are incomplete and providers may be temporarily unavailable. GPS and manual coordinates remain available.

News information

ROAMX checks GDELT and Google News RSS where available. Current-day results are prioritised, with older reports clearly dated. Public news coverage can be incomplete and does not prove an issue exists at the exact selected coordinates.

Community information

Resident observations and needs assessments are user-provided evidence. ROAMX does not independently verify them.

Privacy

Observations and analysis history are stored in this browser's local storage. No account or central database is used by this page.
