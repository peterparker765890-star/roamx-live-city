05 / ROAMX RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

No assumptions. No invented surveys. Evidence before impact.

ROAMX Village Intelligence starts with published evidence, translates findings into practical options, and identifies what still needs to be checked with residents. The next stage is local validation with community members, Gram Panchayat representatives and relevant technical staff.

RESEARCHUse dated, traceable reports and explain what each dataset measures.
VALIDATEAsk residents, inspect local conditions and document limitations.
ACTPrioritise affordable, maintainable interventions.
