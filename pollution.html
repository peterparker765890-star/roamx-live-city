DATA TRANSPARENCY

Understanding the data.

ROAMX Eco Intelligence retrieves modelled atmospheric information through the free Open-Meteo Air Quality API. Its forecast data is based on Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) models.

Important: These are model-based estimates, not direct measurements from a physical monitoring station. Data resolution, coverage and update frequency vary by region. Values may differ from local government monitoring stations.

US AQI is used for the main dashboard. It should not be confused with India's CPCB National AQI scale. This page is an educational awareness tool and does not provide medical advice.

Data attribution: Open-Meteo · Copernicus CAMS.
