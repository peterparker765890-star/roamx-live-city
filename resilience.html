        03 / COMMUNITY RESILIENCE

        Small actions. Safer communities.

        Simple habits can reduce risk and improve disaster readiness.

        01 — Stay informed

        Follow official weather warnings and instructions from local authorities and disaster management agencies.

        02 — Plan evacuation

        Know your nearest safe locations, evacuation routes and a meeting point for your family.

        03 — Support others

        Help children, older adults and people with disabilities access reliable information and safe assistance.
