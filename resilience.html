Practical disaster-preparedness knowledge for everyday life. Learn what to do before, during and after emergencies — and prepare your household before a crisis happens.

Know your evacuation route, keep essential supplies ready, protect important documents and follow official emergency instructions.

⚠ SAFETY FIRST: This page is an educational reference, not a live emergency service. During an emergency, contact the appropriate authorities and follow instructions from local officials. Never delay evacuation to finish reading a guide.

Emergency essentials

Simple actions that can make a difference.

📞
Know emergency numbers

Save 112 and relevant local emergency contacts in your phone.

🎒
Prepare a go-bag

Keep water, food, medicines, torch, batteries and basic supplies ready.

📍
Plan safe meeting points

Choose a family meeting point and an out-of-area contact.

📄
Protect documents

Store copies of IDs, prescriptions and essential records in a waterproof pouch.

🔋
Keep devices charged

Charge phones and power banks; keep a battery-powered radio if available.

📢
Follow official alerts

Use government weather and disaster updates, not unverified forwards.

Disaster safety guides

Review the steps for each hazard and adapt them to local conditions.

🌊

Flood

Heavy rain · Rising water · Flash floods

Floodwater can hide open drains, debris and electrical hazards. Move to higher ground when instructed.

Before
  • Know local flood-prone areas.
  • Keep documents and medicines protected.
  • Prepare to move to higher ground.
During
  • Move away from rising water.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Switch off electricity only if safe.
After
  • Return only when authorities permit.
  • Avoid damaged wires and buildings.
  • Use safe drinking water.
🌀

Cyclone

Strong winds · Coastal storm surge

Follow official cyclone warnings and evacuation orders, especially in coastal and low-lying areas.

Before
  • Secure loose outdoor objects.
  • Charge phones and power banks.
  • Know the nearest safe shelter.
During
  • Stay inside a strong building.
  • Keep away from windows.
  • Do not go outside during a lull.
After
  • Wait for the official all-clear.
  • Avoid fallen power lines.
  • Stay away from the coast until safe.
🏚️

Earthquake

Ground shaking · Structural damage

During shaking, protect yourself first. Do not run through buildings or use lifts.

Before
  • Secure heavy shelves and objects.
  • Identify sturdy cover.
  • Keep shoes and a torch accessible.
During
  • Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from glass and windows.
After
  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Leave damaged buildings carefully.
  • Do not re-enter unsafe structures.
🔥

Fire

Household · Electrical · Building fire

Raise the alarm and evacuate immediately. Never risk your life trying to save belongings.

Before
  • Know two exits where possible.
  • Keep exits and corridors clear.
  • Check smoke alarms regularly.
During
  • Alert others and call 112 or 101.
  • Use stairs, never lifts.
  • Stay low under smoke.
After
  • Stay outside at a safe point.
  • Do not re-enter the building.
  • Seek medical help after smoke exposure.
☀️

Heatwave

Extreme heat · Dehydration · Heat illness

Reduce heat exposure, drink water regularly and take heat-related symptoms seriously.

Before
  • Check official heat warnings.
  • Keep drinking water available.
  • Plan outdoor work for cooler hours.
During
  • Stay in a cool or shaded place.
  • Wear light, loose clothing.
  • Help children, older people and pets stay cool.
After
  • Rest and rehydrate.
  • Seek urgent help for confusion or collapse.
  • Do not leave anyone in a parked vehicle.
⛰️

Landslide

Unstable slopes · Heavy rain · Rockfall

Watch for cracks, tilted trees, unusual sounds or sudden changes in water flow on slopes.

Before
  • Know whether your area is near an unstable slope.
  • Keep drains clear when safe.
  • Know the local evacuation route.
During
  • Move quickly away from the slide path.
  • Do not cross an active landslide area.
  • Follow evacuation instructions immediately.
After
  • Stay away from the slide zone.
  • Avoid damaged roads, bridges and slopes.
  • Report hazards to authorities.
🌊

Tsunami & Coastal Surge

Coastal earthquake · Sudden sea withdrawal · Official warning

If you are near the coast and feel a strong or long earthquake, or notice the sea suddenly receding or surging, move inland or to higher ground immediately. Do not wait to watch the sea.

Before
  • Know your coastal evacuation route.
  • Identify official warning channels and shelters.
  • Keep an emergency kit ready.
During
  • Move inland or uphill immediately.
  • Do not go to the shore to observe waves.
  • Stay away from coastal rivers and estuaries.
After
  • Remain in a safe area until the all-clear.
  • Do not assume the first wave is the largest.
  • Avoid floodwater and damaged structures.
⛈️

Lightning & Thunderstorm

Lightning · Strong wind · Heavy rain

When thunder is heard, move indoors or into a fully enclosed hard-topped vehicle.

Before
  • Check weather warnings.
  • Postpone exposed outdoor activity.
  • Secure loose items outside.
During
  • Stay away from open fields and isolated trees.
  • Avoid water and metal objects outdoors.
  • Do not use wired electrical devices.
After
  • Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going outside.
  • Avoid fallen wires.
  • Report dangerous damage to authorities.
